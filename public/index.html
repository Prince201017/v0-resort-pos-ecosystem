How It Works

1

Guest Orders

Guests browse menu, select items by category, and place orders from their table.

2

Receipt & QR

Customer receives a token with receipt. QR code contains all order details.

3

Staff Scans

Staff scans QR code to load order details into the billing interface.

4

Billing

Staff prepares final bill, adjusts items if needed, and prints for customer.
